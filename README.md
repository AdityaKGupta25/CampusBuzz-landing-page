# CampusBuzz — India's Verified Open Activity Network

Official landing page and interactive platform preview built strictly in alignment with the **CampusBuzz Business Requirements Document (BRD)**.

Leadership / Founder Email: **founder@thecampusbuzz.in**

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Features (Per BRD)

1. **Dual-Mode Experience (Navbar Toggle)**:
   - 🌐 **Layer 1: The Open Feed**: Publicly discoverable without login. Houses Events, Jobs, Live Sports, and Institution Updates with a desktop discovery sidebar.
   - 🔒 **Layer 3: Campus Insider Simulation**: Private college intranet layer for placement drives, student clubs, departmental notices, and sports selections.

2. **Multi-Entity Ecosystem (Not just for college students!)**:
   - 🏛️ **Colleges & Universities** (Paid SaaS from ₹2,999/mo)
   - 🏫 **High Schools (K-12)** (100% Free Forever — Strategic acquisition funnel)
   - 💻 **Tech & Cultural Communities** (Paid SaaS from ₹1,499/mo)
   - 🏢 **Companies & Recruiters** (Paid SaaS from ₹2,999/mo)
   - 🏆 **Standalone Sports Organizers** (Paid SaaS from ₹999/mo)

3. **Core Dilemma Solved: "Activity Happens. Proof Disappears."**:
   - Interactive Before/After comparison demonstrating how WhatsApp screenshots and buried Instagram stories are replaced with a permanent verified ledger.

4. **Interactive 5-in-1 Open Feed**:
   - Pinned **Live Sports Scoreboards** with real-time commentary, spectator count, and live stream modal with interactive fan chat.
   - **Hackathon & Event Cards** with real-time spot trackers and instant registration modal.
   - **Job & Internship Cards** with salary ranges and 1-click verified application modal.
   - **Institution Update Posts** with verified badge stamps and follower engagement.
   - **Desktop Discovery Sidebar** (Live streams, events closing in 48 hours, hiring firms, trending campuses).

5. **Recruiter Talent Discovery Sandbox (BRD Section 10)**:
   - Interactive recruiter sandbox filtering candidates by verified achievements (e.g. *Won 2+ Hackathons*, *10+ Events Attended*, *Sports Captain*).

6. **Passive Verification & Chain of Trust (BRD Section 14)**:
   - Visual step-by-step breakdown showing how zero paperwork is required from students.

7. **Complete SaaS Pricing Matrix (BRD Section 13)**:
   - Monthly and Annual billing options (Save 17% with 2 Months Free).
   - Dedicated pricing tiers for all 5 institution types.
